An ancient, charmed race, pure of heart, strong of limb, long of life, the Dawn Fey dwell in Dawnwood in the heart of the Bloodmarch. To the east lies the Fallows, to the north the Last Northing, to the west the Long Mountains and to the south, the Marish. Aloroth the Fey, the King's brother, is held hostage in Maranor by the Dark Fey.
